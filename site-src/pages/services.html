01 — Custom Home Construction

Distinctive Homes, Concept to Completion

At Berm Inc., we deliver distinctive single-family homes from concept to completion with a focus on quality, transparency, and craftsmanship. Our team manages every stage of the project, from early planning and feasibility analysis to final finishes and occupancy.

We provide site analysis, zoning and permit coordination, pre-construction budgeting, architectural collaboration, project management, and full-scale construction services. Our expertise includes foundations, structural framing, building envelope systems, windows and doors, exterior finishes, interior detailing, and final quality inspections.

Serving Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area — including Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, King Township, Aurora, Newmarket, Mississauga, Brampton, Caledon, Oakville, Burlington, Milton, and Durham Region — Berm Inc. works closely with homeowners, designers, consultants, and trades to deliver custom homes built to the highest standards.

Whether you are planning a new custom residence or a complete rebuild, our goal is to provide a streamlined construction experience with clear communication, reliable execution, and attention to every detail.

02 — Construction Project Management

Organized, Efficient, Accountable

At Berm Inc., we provide comprehensive construction project management services designed to keep every phase of your project organized, efficient, and accountable. From pre-construction planning to final completion, our team coordinates trades, schedules, budgets, materials, and documentation to ensure a smooth construction process.

We manage master project schedules with clearly defined critical-path milestones, helping maintain workflow efficiency and minimize delays. Our team coordinates all major construction trades including framing, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, drywall, finishing, and specialty installations to ensure proper sequencing and site productivity.

Our procurement and lead-time management process helps secure materials early and prevent scheduling disruptions before they occur. We also maintain centralized document control for drawings, RFIs, permits, change orders, inspection records, and project communications, ensuring all stakeholders remain aligned throughout the project.

Clients receive regular progress updates with photo documentation, schedule tracking, and transparent budget reporting. Through live cost monitoring and forecasting, Berm Inc. helps minimize unexpected costs and provides greater financial clarity from start to finish.

With a focus on communication, coordination, and proactive management, we deliver projects with greater efficiency, quality control, and confidence at every stage of construction.

03 — Interior Finishes & Architectural Details

The Details Define the Home

At Berm Inc., we believe exceptional homes are defined by the quality of their finishes and the precision of their details. Our team works closely with homeowners, designers, suppliers, and fabricators to create cohesive interior and exterior environments that combine functionality, craftsmanship, and timeless design.

We assist with material specification and finish selection, including flooring, tile, natural stone, cabinetry finishes, millwork species, paint palettes, and architectural details. Through our network of showroom partners and manufacturers, we help source premium plumbing fixtures, lighting systems, hardware, and specialty finishes that align with the project vision and budget.

Our services also include custom cabinetry and millwork solutions for kitchens, bathrooms, built-ins, feature walls, storage systems, and architectural woodwork. Every finish and hardware selection is coordinated to maintain consistency across the entire home.

In addition, Berm Inc. specializes in European aluminum and uPVC glazing systems, including premium windows, doors, and curtain wall solutions. We support specification, sourcing, import coordination, and installation management for high-performance architectural glazing products tailored to modern residential construction.

From initial selections to final installation, we focus on delivering refined finishes, cohesive detailing, and elevated living environments built around quality and craftsmanship.

04 — Renovations & Additions

Modernize While Respecting the Original

We specialize in high-quality renovations and additions that modernize living spaces while respecting the original character of the home. Our team delivers carefully planned construction solutions that improve functionality, increase property value, and elevate overall design.

We provide second-storey additions, rear extensions, and full structural integration services designed to seamlessly connect new and existing spaces. For interior transformations, we manage open-concept reconfigurations including load-bearing wall removal supported by engineered LVL or structural steel beam systems.

Our renovation expertise also includes complete gut renovations with upgraded mechanical, electrical, plumbing, insulation, and building systems while maintaining or restoring key architectural features where desired.

For lower-level living spaces, Berm Inc. delivers basement underpinning, walkout construction, waterproofing, and luxury basement finishing tailored for modern residential use. We also provide premium kitchen and bathroom renovations with custom cabinetry, refined finishes, upgraded fixtures, and detailed craftsmanship throughout.

From design coordination to final finishing, our renovation projects are managed with precision, transparency, and attention to every construction detail.
