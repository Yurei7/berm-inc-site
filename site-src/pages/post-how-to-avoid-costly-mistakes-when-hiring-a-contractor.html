How to Avoid Costly Mistakes When Hiring a Contractor

May 15Omid Rostami1 min read

Hiring the right contractor is one of the most important decisions in any renovation or custom home project.

Before signing a contract, always check:

  • Clear scope of work and pricing
  • Insurance & WSIB coverage
  • Experience and past project quality
  • Timeline and communication process
  • Exterior work details (windows, doors, waterproofing, cladding)

Most construction problems happen when decisions are made based only on the lowest price without reviewing details properly.

Poor exterior installation can lead to leaks, energy loss, and expensive repairs later.

A professional contractor provides clear communication, organized planning, and transparent pricing.

Good construction is not just about building — it is about protecting your long-term investment.
