What It Costs to Build a Custom Home in the GTA (2026)

August 24, 2026Omid Rostami6 min read

Every custom home budget conversation starts with the same question: what does it cost per square foot? The honest answer is that a per-square-foot number is an average of decisions you have not made yet — but published 2026 ranges are still useful, because they tell you what ballpark to plan in before you talk to a builder.

Published 2026 ranges for Ontario and the GTA

Across publicly published 2026 Ontario cost guides, custom home construction generally falls into these bands:

What the number does not include

Per-square-foot figures describe construction of the home. They generally do not include:

  • Land — purchase price, and any costs to make the lot buildable
  • Development charges and municipal fees — these vary by municipality and by project
  • Design and engineering fees — architects, structural, mechanical, energy modelling
  • Utility connections — hydro, gas, water, sanitary, storm
  • Site works — excavation, grading, retaining, driveways, landscaping
  • Interim financing costs and HST treatment
  • Contingency — the line item nobody wants to budget and everybody needs

What actually drives cost per square foot

Square footage is one variable among several. In practice, these move a budget more:

  • Site and foundation conditions. High water table, rock, poor soil or a tight urban lot can change excavation and foundation strategy significantly.
  • Structural design. Long spans, cantilevers, and large open spaces cost more than a conventional framed layout of the same size.
  • Windows and doors. For a high-performance home this is often the single largest specification line — frame material, glazing package and size all matter.
  • Envelope targets. Continuous insulation, airtightness detailing and thermal-bridge-free construction add work up front and pay back in comfort and energy use.
  • Mechanical systems. Heating, cooling and balanced ventilation designed as a system cost more than minimum-code equipment.
  • Finish level. Two homes with identical footprints can differ by hundreds of thousands of dollars in cabinetry, tile, millwork, fixtures and lighting.

Why the lowest bid usually ends up costing more

A low number is only meaningful if the scope is identical. When two bids differ by a large margin on the same drawings, the difference is usually found in what is not included: envelope detailing, insulation depth, window specification, site supervision, or allowances that will be exceeded. Those gaps reappear later as change orders — after the budget has already been approved and the walls are up.

Common questions

What is the average cost per square foot to build a custom home in Ontario in 2026?

Published 2026 Ontario cost guides quote roughly $300–$600 per square foot for custom construction, with luxury custom homes in Toronto and the GTA commonly reaching $900 per square foot and above.

Does that price include the lot?

No. Published per-square-foot figures generally cover construction of the home itself, not land acquisition, development charges or utility connection fees.

Why do custom home costs vary so much?

Site conditions, foundation and excavation requirements, window and door specifications, insulation and airtightness targets, mechanical systems, and finish level all move the number — typically more than square footage does.
