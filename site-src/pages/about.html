About Berm

25+ years of construction and project management experience, brought home to Ontario.

Our Foundation

Built on Technical Expertise and Hands-On Knowledge

With over 25 years of experience in construction and project management, Berm Inc. is built on a strong foundation of technical expertise, leadership, and hands-on industry knowledge.

Prior to establishing Berm Inc. in Canada in 2016, our founder managed and delivered large-scale industrial and infrastructure projects internationally, including petrochemical and oil refinery facilities, heavy concrete process buildings, piping systems, geotextile and geomembrane installation for evaporation ponds, and dike wall construction.

Today, Berm Inc. focuses on residential construction and renovation projects across Ontario, bringing the same commitment to quality, professionalism, and workmanship to every project.

As an HCRA-certified builder in Ontario, we are dedicated to delivering reliable construction solutions with integrity, attention to detail, and client satisfaction at the core of our work.

How We Work

Our Process

At Berm Inc., we believe that a well-defined process is key to successful construction. Our approach integrates client collaboration, technical planning, and execution excellence to ensure every project meets our high standards.

01 — Process

Discovery Consultation

We begin with an on-site consultation to understand your vision, assess feasibility, discuss budget expectations, and establish a realistic project timeline.

Pre-Construction Planning

Working alongside your architect or our trusted design partners, we refine drawings, coordinate budgeting, define scope, and prepare a detailed construction agreement.

Permit & Procurement

Our team manages permit submissions, material selections, trade coordination, and procurement of long-lead items including windows, millwork, and specialty products.

Construction Execution

From site mobilization to final inspections, every stage is professionally managed through scheduled milestones, progress updates, quality control, and transparent communication.

Pre-Occupancy Walkthrough

Before completion, we conduct a detailed walkthrough with you to identify and resolve any outstanding items prior to final handover.

Post-Occupancy Support

We continue supporting our clients after move-in with workmanship warranty coverage, ongoing service, and responsive post-construction assistance by Berm Inc.
