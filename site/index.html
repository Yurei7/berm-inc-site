The Berm Envelope

One Continuous Protective Layer Around Your Home.

A high-performance building envelope separates the controlled indoor environment from Ontario's changing outdoor climate. At Berm, we pay particular attention to:

  • Continuous Insulation — less heat loss through walls, roofs, and foundations.
  • Airtight Construction — a continuous air-control layer around the building.
  • Thermal-Bridge Reduction — detailing where structure could conduct heat.
  • High-Performance Windows & Doors — installed as part of the envelope, not openings in the wall.
  • Water & Moisture Management — drainage planes, flashing, membranes, and transitions done right.

The Connections

Details Matter

Performance Is Won at the Connections.

A wall can contain excellent insulation and still underperform if the transitions are poorly detailed. That is why Berm pays close attention to locations such as:

  • Window-to-wall connections
  • Door thresholds
  • Foundation-to-wall transitions
  • Wall-to-roof connections
  • Balconies and structural penetrations
  • Cladding attachments
  • Air-barrier transitions
  • Flashing and drainage details

These small areas can have a major effect on airtightness, moisture control, thermal bridging, and long-term durability.

The details you cannot see are often the details that matter most.

Windows as Part of the Envelope

A High-Performance Window Is Only as Good as Its Installation.

Windows are among the most technically important components of the building envelope. Berm considers the complete connection between the window and wall, including:

Thermal Continuity Air-Barrier Continuity Water Management Structural Support Exterior Weather Sealing Interior Airtight Sealing

This approach helps reduce drafts, condensation risk, heat loss, and thermal bridging around the opening.
