About Berm

25+ years of construction and project management experience, brought home to Ontario.

Our Foundation

Built on Technical Expertise and Hands-On Knowledge

With over 25 years of experience in construction and project management, Berm Inc. is built on a strong foundation of technical expertise, leadership, and hands-on industry knowledge.

Prior to establishing Berm Inc. in Canada in 2016, our founder managed and delivered large-scale industrial and infrastructure projects internationally, including petrochemical and oil refinery facilities, heavy concrete process buildings, piping systems, geotextile and geomembrane installation for evaporation ponds, and dike wall construction.

Today, Berm Inc. focuses on residential construction and renovation projects across Ontario, bringing the same commitment to quality, professionalism, and workmanship to every project.

As an HCRA-licensed builder in Ontario, we are dedicated to delivering reliable construction solutions with integrity, attention to detail, and client satisfaction at the core of our work.

How We Build
