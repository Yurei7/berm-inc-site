03 — Process

What Building in Vaughan Actually Involves

A custom home in Vaughan needs a building permit before construction starts. The City's Building Standards department reviews applications for new single-family dwellings, and publishes a target processing time of approximately 10 business days once a complete application is submitted — with more complex projects taking longer if zoning relief or additional reviews are needed.

That is why we treat the pre-construction phase as part of the build, not paperwork before it. Drawings, engineering, zoning review and permit submission are coordinated up front, so construction starts with an approved, buildable plan rather than a set of questions.

On the construction side, Vaughan is a cold-climate market. The details that decide how a home feels in February — continuous insulation, airtight construction, thermal-bridge-free transitions and properly installed windows — are the ones specified and inspected on site rather than assumed. Our building approach covers how that work is sequenced and verified.

And because we are an HCRA-licensed Vendor and Builder (Licence No. B60719), homeowners can verify our licensing status independently in the Ontario Builder Directory.
